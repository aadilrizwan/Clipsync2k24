# ClipSync Documentation

Welcome to **ClipSync**! ClipSync is a video recording and sharing platform designed to capture, organize, and share high-quality video content with teams, clients, and followers. Use ClipSync to create workspaces, collaborate with teams, record and share videos, and choose from paid and free plans to fit your needs.

## Getting Started

1. **Sign Up**  
   To get started with ClipSync, visit clipsync and sign up using your email, Google account, or other available methods.

2. **Login**  
   Once registered, log in to access all the features and start creating and sharing videos.

## Creating Workspaces

Workspaces in ClipSync allow you to organize your videos and manage team collaboration in one central location.

1. **Navigate to the Workspace Section**  
   From the dashboard, select **"Workspaces"**.

2. **Create a New Workspace**  
   - Click on **"Create Workspace"**.
   - Enter a **Workspace Name** and **Description**.
   - Select visibility settings:
     - **Public**: Anyone with the link can view.
     - **Private**: Restricted to selected team members.

3. **Organize Workspace Folders**  
   Add folders within the workspace to categorize and manage video content by project or topic.

4. **Edit or Delete Workspaces**  
   Access settings in each workspace to update details, rename, or delete.

## Adding Team Members

1. **Invite Team Members**  
   - Open your workspace and click on **"Team Members"**.
   - Select **"Invite Members"** and enter the email addresses of the individuals you want to add.
   - Assign a role:
     - **Admin**: Full access to all workspace settings and videos.
     - **Member**: Access to view and add content within the workspace.
   
2. **Manage Invitations**  
   Pending invitations will appear in the **Team Members** section until the invite is accepted.

3. **Edit Member Permissions**  
   Change roles or remove members by clicking on their profile within the workspace.

## Recording Videos

1. **Start a New Recording**  
   - Go to **Record** in the dashboard.
   - Choose to record:
     - **Screen Only**
     - **Camera Only**
     - **Screen + Camera** (split-screen mode)

2. **Customize Recording Settings**  
   - Select the screen, tab, or app window you want to record.
   - Enable microphone and camera permissions if needed.

3. **Pause, Resume, and Stop Recording**  
   Use the toolbar options to control recording.

4. **Save and Name Recording**  
   After stopping, give your recording a title, description, and tags to organize it in your workspace.

## Sharing Video Links

1. **Generate a Shareable Link**  
   - Open a video from your workspace.
   - Click **"Share"** and select **"Generate Link"**.
   - Customize link settings:
     - **View-Only** or **Edit** permissions
     - **Expiration Date** (if you want it to expire after a certain date)

2. **Embed or Copy Link**  
   Copy the generated link to share via email, message, or embed it in other platforms.

3. **Share with Team Members**  
   - For workspace members, you can directly notify them by selecting **"Notify Team Members"**.

## Downloading the Desktop Plugin

The ClipSync desktop plugin provides enhanced recording features and system access for improved quality.

1. **Download Plugin**  
   - From the **Settings** page, go to **Downloads**.
   - Select **Download for Desktop** (available for Windows, Mac, and Linux).

2. **Install Plugin**  
   Run the downloaded file and follow the on-screen installation instructions.

3. **Log In on Desktop**  
   Open the desktop plugin and log in to access ClipSync’s features directly from your computer.

## Selecting Resolution

Choose the optimal resolution for recording quality based on your device and network capabilities.

1. **Adjust Resolution**  
   Before starting a recording, open **Settings** in the recording toolbar.
   
2. **Resolution Options**  
   - **480p**: Standard quality, best for slower networks.
   - **720p**: HD quality, suitable for general use.
   - **1080p**: Full HD, ideal for high-quality screen recording.
   - **4K**: Ultra HD, best for professional-quality video but requires high-performance hardware.

## Plans and Pricing

ClipSync offers a range of plans to fit your needs, including a free version and paid plans with additional features.

1. **Free Plan**  
   - Access to basic features such as limited recording time, basic sharing, and standard resolution options.
   - Up to 5 team members per workspace.

2. **Paid Plan**  
   - Extended recording times, advanced sharing and security options, and access to 1080p and 4K resolutions.
   - Custom branding, priority support, and up to 50 team members per workspace.

3. **Upgrading Plans**  
   - Visit **Settings > Billing** to view plans and upgrade your account.
   - Choose monthly or annual billing.
